Research vision

Care informed by lived experience.

I study what patients report between clinical encounters and how those signals can help us recognize changing health earlier.

My research develops patient-centered AI that transforms symptoms, concerns, treatment experiences, and behaviors into actionable information for earlier detection and personalized care. I combine large language models, natural language processing, and longitudinal machine learning across patient messages, clinical conversations, audio, and emerging data sources.

The goal is not simply to predict an outcome. It is to understand how health evolves over time, communicate risk meaningfully, and support decisions that reflect each patient’s needs, preferences, and daily experience.
